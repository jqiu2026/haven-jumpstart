# Blinded

**Blinded** is a horror survival game where your goal is to survive ghosts for three minutes.

## Objective

- Stay alive until the timer reaches zero.
- Avoid contact with ghosts as they track your position.

## Controls

- **Move Left:** `A` or `Left Arrow`
- **Move Right:** `D` or `Right Arrow`
- **Jump:** `W`, `Space`, or `Up Arrow`
